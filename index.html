The Chalet

Spacious luxury accommodation in the Tyrolean Alps

Sophisticated Alpine Living

Five beautifully appointed bedrooms sleeping up to 10 guests. Open-plan living spaces with vaulted wooden ceilings, designer kitchen, modern fireplaces, and floor-to-ceiling windows framing the Zillertal Alps. Four luxury bathrooms including spa-style corner tubs and rain showers.

Prime Ski Location

Direct access to the Eggalm cable car - literally opposite the chalet. Ski-to-door convenience with dedicated boot room. Minutes from Hintertux Glacier, Austria's only year-round skiing destination. Summer brings hiking trails, tennis courts, and alpine meadows.

Luxury Amenities

Spacious balconies on every level with mountain vistas. Private parking. High-speed WiFi. Professional-grade kitchen with wine storage. Outdoor fireplace and sun terrace. Located in Lanersbach's best-rated area with a perfect 10.0 location score.
